# JUE-0013 — No free rounds or broken ratios

Source: spike JUE-0008, proposed job 3; findings F3, F4.

Goal: every round has at least one misspelling, and accentFixer keeps its 80/20 ratio.

## Pending
- [ ] Drop words without a distractor and oversample so games still have 15 rounds.
- [ ] wordsRain never drops a word that has no misspelling.
- [ ] accentFixer draws until it has 8 accented words, or prefilters the accented ones.
- [ ] accentFixer's results screen shows what the player chose, not «brazo → brazo».

## Notes
- Depends on JUE-0011 (same pool code).

Done =
- a `useSpellTower` test: a 1-option round is never shown (en beginner included), and with a pool
  where half the words have no distractor the game still has 15 rounds;
- a `useWordsRain` test: with a pool that includes words without a distractor, none of them falls, and
  every correct word that falls has at least one misspelling in the sequence;
- a test: in beginner, ≥ 7 of 10 words have an accent when the pool has them;
- an accentFixer UI test: if the player marks an accent on «brazo», the final screen shows their choice
  against the correct one («brázo → brazo»), not «brazo  brazo».
