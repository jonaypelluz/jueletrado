# JUE-0011 — Correct word pools per game, level and locale

Source: spike JUE-0008 (summary in `ops/jobs/memory.md`), proposed job 1; findings F1, F2, F7, F8.

Goal: every game session gets words inside its own length window and its own locale, and playing
again brings new words.

## Pending
- [ ] `WORD_GROUP_FETCH_CONFIG` (or `prefetchWordGroups`) uses each game's length window per level.
- [ ] `getSessionWords` drops words outside the window.
- [ ] Pools and `SELECTED_DAY_WORD` carry the locale in their key (or are invalidated when the locale
      changes by URL).
- [ ] spellTower, wordsRain and wordFinder fetch new words on play-again and on level change.
- [ ] wordsRain interleaves correct words and misspellings at random, not in sequence.
- [ ] wordFinder ends the game when it runs out of words.
- [ ] The word of the day uses `WORDS_DAILY` (≥ 4 letters), or that key is removed.

## Notes
- No dependencies. JUE-0013 builds on the same pool code.
- Word of the day: JUE-0012 cleans the beginner list it draws from.

Done =
- one test per game shows that, after choosing a level, the first session respects the window;
- a second-game test with no repeated words for spellTower, wordsRain and wordFinder;
- a `useWordsRain` test with mocked `Math.random`: the word after a correct one is not always its own
  misspelling, and two games in a row do not fall into the same sequence;
- a `useWordFinder` test: solving or failing the last word ends the game (countdown stopped, Play
  visible);
- a `loadDailyWordForLocale` test: with an es word stored, asking for `en` returns a word from the en
  list (the `SELECTED_DAY_WORD` key carries the locale);
- one test each for `useSpellTower`, `useWordsRain` and `useWordFinder`: with the hook mounted,
  changing `gameLevel` refetches words for the new level, and the next game only has words from that
  level's list;
- opening `/en/` after es loads en words (test or browser QA);
- the word of the day never has fewer than 4 letters.
